About ULMFlix

ULMFlix is a movie discovery and entertainment website designed to help visitors explore films, ratings, genres, recommendations and editorial information in one place.

What we publish

We aim to publish original descriptions, reviews, guides and entertainment information. Movie names, artwork and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.

Our goal
